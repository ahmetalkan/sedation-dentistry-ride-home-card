Who drives you home after sedation dentistry? A planning card

For many forms of dental sedation, the appointment is only half the plan: someone else has to get you home and stay with the start of the recovery. This card is a pre-visit checklist so that the ride, the fasting window and the first evening are arranged before the chair, not after.

The American Dental Association’s oral-health topic on anesthesia and sedation is the profession’s overview of the methods dentists use. MedlinePlus’s encyclopedia article on conscious sedation describes a drug-induced state in which you can still respond, and it is one of the pages patients are often handed when they ask what “sedation” will feel like. The American Society of Anesthesiologists publishes a statement on the continuum of depth of sedation, which is the standard language clinicians use to distinguish minimal sedation, moderate sedation, deep sedation and general anesthesia. Which level you are booked for is a question for your own dentist; this card only helps you plan around the answer.

The ride home is not optional for many techniques

A direct guide, can I drive after sedation dentistry, is the page to read before you assume you can take yourself home. Pair it with how long sedation lasts and with sedation dentistry recovery, so the person who is collecting you also knows how long they may need to stay and what a normal first evening looks like.

Write the dentist’s actual instructions on this card. If the practice says you may not drive, sign papers, or cook that day, treat that as the rule even if you “feel fine” in the car park.

Planning card

ItemFilled in before the visit
Which depth of sedation is planned (use the dentist’s words, or the ASA continuum terms if they use them)?

Who stays with you for the first hours at home
Fasting and medicine instructions (copied from the practice sheet)
When the practice says you may drive again
Who to call after hours if bleeding, breathing trouble, or unexpected sleepiness appears

If you cannot arrange a driver, say so when you book, not on the morning of the appointment; some techniques or appointment lengths may need to change. Q Sedation Dentistry is a directory of clinics that offer sedation, which is useful once the practical side of the day is settled.

This card does not tell you that any particular level of sedation is safe for you, and it is not permission to drive. Follow the written instructions from the dentist who is treating you.
